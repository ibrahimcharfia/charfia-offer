// Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I pay any fees or commissions?

No. There are no commissions and no fees. We pay all of your closing costs.

How do you calculate the offer?

We look at what similar updated homes nearby sold for, subtract the repairs the house needs and our costs, and leave a fair margin. We walk you through the numbers.

Are you real estate agents?

No. We are a direct buyer. We do not list your house. We buy it ourselves.

How fast can you close?

In as little as 14 days through a licensed Texas title company. If you need more time, you pick a later date.

Am I obligated if I request an offer?

No. The offer is free and there is no obligation to accept it.

Do I need to repair or clean the house?

No. We buy as-is in any condition. Take what you want and leave the rest.

Can you buy a house with tenants?

Yes. We can buy rental properties with tenants in place.

I am behind on payments or have liens. Can you still buy?

Often yes. Mortgage payoffs, back taxes and liens are paid from the sale at the title company. The sooner you reach out, the more options you have.
